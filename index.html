Privacy Policy – PDF Kit

Developer: wizard-developer
Last updated: 16 November 2026 (app version 1.3.1)

Short version

Your files stay on your phone unless you choose a cloud feature (Google Drive backup, share as link) or share them yourself. PDF Kit has no ads, no account of its own and no server of its own. The only things sent automatically are crash reports and basic usage statistics (which tools are opened), never your files or their names, and you can turn both off in Settings.

Your files

Scanning, creating, merging, compressing, converting, signing, filling forms, reading aloud, translating and every other tool run on your device. Files you pick are read only to do the task you asked for. Results are kept in the app's private storage until you delete them or uninstall the app. We (the developer) never receive your files.

Secure vault

Files you move to the Secure vault are encrypted on your phone with a key kept in your phone's secure hardware (Android Keystore). They are opened with your fingerprint, face or screen lock. Vault files are never backed up or uploaded and are deleted if you uninstall the app.

Crash reports and usage statistics

If the app crashes, it sends a technical crash report using Google Firebase Crashlytics so we can fix the problem. A report contains the error details, app version, phone model, Android version and a random installation ID. It does not contain your files, file names or their content.

The app also sends basic usage statistics using Google Analytics for Firebase, so we can see which tools are used and which ones fail: the name of the screen or tool opened (for example "Compress"), whether a tool finished or showed an error, roughly how long it took, how many PDFs were added to My Files, plus the app version, phone model, Android version, country and a random app-instance ID. It does not include your files, file names, file content or anything you type. The advertising ID is not collected and the data is not used for ads or shared with advertisers.

You can turn off both crash reports and usage statistics in Settings › Send crash reports and usage statistics. See Firebase privacy.

Google Drive backup (optional)

If you turn on backup in Settings, you choose a Google account on your phone and allow PDF Kit to use a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive. PDF Kit then uploads your PDFs from My Files, your starred files and last-read pages, and your saved signature to that folder, and downloads them when you restore. The data goes directly between your phone and Google; it never passes through any server of ours, and we cannot see it. You can delete the backup at any time in Settings › Delete backup, and remove PDF Kit's access in your Google Account (Security › Third-party connections).

Share as link (optional)

For large PDFs you can choose Share as link. The file is uploaded to a folder called "PDF Kit" in your own Google Drive and set so that anyone with the link can view it. You decide who gets the link, and you can delete the file from Google Drive at any time.

Google user data

PDF Kit asks only for these Google Drive permissions: drive.appdata (its own hidden backup folder) and drive.file (only files PDF Kit itself creates). It cannot see your other Drive files. Google data is used only to provide backup, restore and share-as-link for you; it is not shared with anyone, not used for ads, and not used to train AI models. PDF Kit's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

PDF from link and web pages

When you paste or share a link, PDF Kit connects to that website to download the PDF or show the page you asked for. That website sees a normal request from your phone, like when you open it in a browser. Nothing else is sent.

Scanning, text recognition and translation

The document scanner and text recognition (OCR) use Google ML Kit through Google Play services. Translation uses Google ML Kit on your phone; each language (about 30 MB) is downloaded once from Google, then translation works offline. Images and text are processed on your device and are not sent to Google or to us.

Read aloud

Read aloud uses your phone's own text-to-speech engine. The text is processed on your phone.

Your signature

If you draw or import a signature, it is saved on your phone so you can reuse it, and included in your Google Drive backup only if you turn backup on.

Permissions

Internet (crash reports, usage statistics and the cloud features above, only when you use them), network state, and fingerprint/biometric (Secure vault). PDF Kit does not ask for camera, storage, contacts or location permissions.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If a future version adds advertising, purchases or new data uses, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
